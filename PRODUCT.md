# Boardwright — product one-pager

**What it is:** Draft board and card layouts, export a build-ready spec

**Who it is for:** Someone with a board game half-designed who wants a working
digital prototype, and intends to have a coding agent build it rather than
writing the engine themselves.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** At a desk, late, with the game already sketched on paper. They
know the zones and the turn order in their head; what they do not have is
anything an agent could act on. One session here should end with a file they can
drop into a repo.

**What done looks like:** A downloaded bundle whose open-questions list they have
either answered or knowingly accepted. Not a pretty board: a spec whose gaps are
named out loud.
